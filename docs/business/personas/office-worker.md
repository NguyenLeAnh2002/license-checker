# Persona: Office Worker

## Summary
An employee in a corporate environment who uses Windows and Office daily for their job. They have minimal technical expertise and rely on simple, clear notifications to stay informed about their machine's license status.

## Goals
- Know whether their own Windows and Office licenses are legitimate or cracked
- Understand their compliance status without having to investigate or check systems actively

## Pain Points
- Currently unaware of their actual license status — don't know if their machine is compliant or at risk
- Would be caught off-guard if they learned a license was cracked during an audit

## Context of Use
- **Frequency:** Passive — only engages when receiving a notification about license status
- **Device:** Primary desktop or laptop (work machine)
- **Environment:** At their desk, during normal work hours
- **Trigger:** Receives pop-up notifications when license status changes; does not actively check a dashboard or portal

## Technical Proficiency
**Low** — Minimal technical comfort with compliance or licensing concepts. Requires:
- Very simple, jargon-free language in notifications
- Clear status indicators (e.g., "Your Windows is Legitimate" vs. "Your Office is Cracked")
- No need to understand the underlying mechanism or troubleshooting steps; just the fact

## Relationship to Vision/PRD
- **Vision Goal G-2:** Alert end users on their machines when license status changes
- **Epic-5:** Client-Side Notifications — this persona is the direct user of this capability
- UX implication: Notifications must be simple, non-technical, and immediately understandable without additional context or actions required
